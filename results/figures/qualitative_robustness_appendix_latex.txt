  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{qualitative_robustness_appendix.pdf}
  \caption{\textbf{Qualitative robustness analysis on held-out corruptions.}
  For each example we show the clean left image, the corrupted input, and the corruption-induced disparity drift
  $|\Delta d| = |d_{\mathrm{corr}} - d_{\mathrm{clean}}|$ of the clean-only baseline (E0) and of the full model;
  this is a prediction change, not an error with respect to ground truth.
  Both drift maps use the same colour scale (clipped at 50\,px); the insets give the image mean and the sky-region mean of $|\Delta d|$.
  Large baseline changes are concentrated in evidence-poor regions such as sky and low-texture areas, often in
  blocks aligned with the inference tiles, whereas the full model substantially reduces these failures under fog,
  frost, and snow. Fog, frost and snow are never seen during training. Residual drift of the full model remains visible in the fogged sky (top row).
  Examples correspond to fog scene 0038/frame 0072, frost scene 0045/frame 0066, and snow scene 0047/frame 0222,
  all at severity 3.}
  \label{fig:qualitative_robustness_appendix}
\end{figure*}
